How an injury case works

From your first call to your settlement

Most people have never hired a lawyer before. Here is what to expect.

1

Free evaluation

Call or send the form. An attorney reviews what happened and tells you plainly whether you have a case.

2

We take over

We notify the insurers, gather the police report, medical records and evidence, and handle every call so you can focus on recovery.

3

Treatment & build-up

We help you get the medical care you need and document your injuries, lost wages and expenses as the case develops.

4

Resolution

We negotiate for full value and are prepared to file suit and take your case to trial if the insurer won't be fair.
